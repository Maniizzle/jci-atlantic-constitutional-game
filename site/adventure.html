How to play
  • You play a new member rising from applicant to President of JCI Atlantic, across 12 scenes.
  • Each scene gives you three choices. Pick what the constitution says you should do.
  • A wrong choice is a slip-up. You'll see what the constitution says, then rewind the scene and choose again.
  • Scoring: fewer slip-ups is better. Ties on the leaderboard go to the faster time.
  • Official run: only your first full run counts for the leaderboard. Every run after that is practice.
  • Leaving mid-run? Your official run waits for you and continues where you stopped.
