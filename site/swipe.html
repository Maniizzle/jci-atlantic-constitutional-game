How to play
  • Read the statement about the JCI Atlantic constitution and decide if it's true or false.
  • Swipe right for true, left for false. You can also tap the True and False buttons, or use the → and ← keys.
  • After each answer the card shows what the constitution actually says, with the article it comes from.
  • Scoring: 1 point per correct card. Your rank at the end goes from Intending Member up to President.
  • Official rounds: your first quick round and your first full round each month count for the leaderboard. Replays are practice.
  • Leaving mid-round? Your official round waits for you and continues where you stopped.
