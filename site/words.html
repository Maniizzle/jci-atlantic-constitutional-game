The Constitution Crossword

How to play
  • Pick a word: tap a square or a clue. Tap the same square again to switch between across and down.
  • Type the answer. When a word is right, its clue gets a ✓ and shows the article it comes from.
  • Check answers marks wrong letters in red. Each wrong letter costs 1 point.
  • Reveal this word fills in the word you picked. That word scores 0, but its letters can help with crossing words.
  • Scoring: 10 points per word you solve yourself. Your first finish of each puzzle is your official score; after that it's practice.
